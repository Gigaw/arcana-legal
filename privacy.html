ARCANA

Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

This policy explains how the Arcana mobile application (“Arcana”, “we”, “us”) handles information. Arcana is a tarot journal and AI-assisted interpretation service. We do not sell personal information.

Information we process

  • Account information. Arcana begins with an anonymous account. If you connect Apple or Google, we process the account identifier and information made available for authentication, which may include your email address.
  • Reading content. We store submitted questions, selected spreads, drawn cards, generated interpretations, follow-up messages, and dates to provide your reading history.
  • Daily Card activity. We process your account identifier, local date, time zone, revealed card, and requested personalization.
  • Subscription information. We process product identifiers, purchase and renewal status, entitlements, and expiration dates. Apple or Google processes payment details; Arcana does not receive your full payment-card information.
  • Technical and usage information. We may process app version, platform, locale, feature events, performance data, and crash details to operate, secure, and improve Arcana. Reading questions and interpretations are excluded from analytics and crash reports.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, synchronize content, provide readings and Daily Card features, process subscriptions, restore purchases, enforce plan limits, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

AI processing

When you request an interpretation, Arcana sends the question, language, selected spread, and drawn cards through our server to an AI service provider. We do not intentionally send your name, email address, or payment information with that request. AI output is validated by our server before it is saved or shown.

Service providers

We use Supabase for authentication, database, and server functions; OpenAI for AI-assisted interpretations; RevenueCat and the applicable app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) for subscriptions; and Apple or Google when you use their sign-in services. Limited diagnostics or usage events may be processed by Sentry or PostHog when enabled. Providers process information under their own terms and privacy commitments.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and reading content while your account is active or until you delete it. You can permanently delete your Arcana account from the app or by following the steps on our account deletion page; this removes the account and its user-owned readings from our active systems. Deleting Arcana does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Limited security, transaction, or backup records may remain where required by law or retained by service providers under their policies.

Your choices

You may use Arcana anonymously, link an account, delete readings, restore purchases, or delete your account in the app. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Providers may process information in countries other than yours, subject to applicable safeguards.

Children

Arcana is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country.

Changes

We may update this policy as Arcana changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required.
