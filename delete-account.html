Delete your Arcana account

Arcana — tarot journal by PurplePig

You can permanently delete your Arcana account and its data at any time, either in the app or by emailing us.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Arcana.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account (or Delete data if you have not signed in).
  4. Confirm with Delete everything.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app, email gigolaevigor1999@gmail.com with the subject “Arcana account deletion” from the email address of the Apple or Google account you used to sign in to Arcana. We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your Arcana account and its sign-in link to Apple or Google.
  • Your readings, including questions, drawn cards, interpretations, and follow-ups.
  • Your Daily Card history and personalizations.
  • Unfinished draws stored on the device you delete from.

What may be kept

Subscription transaction records held by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat, and limited security or backup records, may be retained where required by law or under those providers’ policies. Diagnostics and usage events are not linked to your reading content.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
